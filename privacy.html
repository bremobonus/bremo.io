Privacy

Your privacy.

A plain-language summary and a starting point. Before Bremo collects real personal or health information from the public, this notice is to be reviewed by a qualified privacy lawyer and finalised for the places we operate.

What you share when looking for care is sensitive. We treat it as confidential and collect only what we need to help.

What we collect

  • What you tell us when looking for care — the kind of support you'd like, what's going on, your preferences, where you're based, and how to reach you.
  • From practitioners applying to join — name, contact, profession, licensing body and registration number, and availability.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the site safely.

How we use it

  • To understand what you need and introduce you to a suitable, verified professional.
  • To confirm that practitioners are licensed and in good standing.
  • To contact you about your match or application.
  • To keep the service safe.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't share what you tell us beyond what's needed to arrange your care, and only with your knowledge.
  • We don't use your health information for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Under applicable privacy law — in Canada, PIPEDA and provincial health-privacy laws such as Ontario's PHIPA — you have rights over your personal information. To make a request, email privacy@bremo.io.

How we protect it

We limit what we collect, restrict who can see it, and use reasonable safeguards. No system is perfectly secure, and this notice will be updated as our safeguards are finalised.

This template is not legal advice. Bremo is early-stage; the sections noted for review must be completed with qualified legal counsel before real personal or health data is handled.
