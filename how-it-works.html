How it works

Finding care shouldn't be another full-time job.

Bremo does the hard part — understanding what you need and finding the right professional for it — so the only thing you have to do is show up.

1

Share what's going on

A short, private intake in plain language. Tell us as much or as little as you're comfortable with.

2

We understand & match

Bremo interprets your needs, preferences, language, budget and availability, then shortlists verified professionals who genuinely fit.

3

Meet & begin care

Review your match, book an intro, and start — online or in person. If the fit isn't right, we re-match at no cost.

Where the AI helps — and where humans stay in charge

Bremo uses AI to do what software is good at: reading your intake carefully, asking sensible follow-ups, and surfacing the professionals whose specialty and approach fit your situation. That's it.

AI does not deliver your care, diagnose you, or make clinical decisions. Care is provided by licensed human professionals. A human care coordinator reviews sensitive matches, and anything that looks urgent is routed to crisis resources — not into a queue.

How we keep it safe

  • Credential verification. Every professional's licence and standing is confirmed with their regulatory college or board before they can be matched.
  • Crisis routing. If your intake signals immediate risk, we point you straight to 988 and emergency services instead of matching.
  • Privacy by design. Your intake is confidential, minimised to what's needed, and handled in line with Canadian privacy law. See our Privacy Notice.
  • You stay in control. You choose your professional, what you share, and whether to continue.

Bremo is a matching service, not a medical or emergency provider. It doesn't replace your doctor, a diagnosis, or 911.
