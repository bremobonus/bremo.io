If you're in immediate danger, call 911 now.

Bremo is a matching service and can't respond to emergencies. The services below are free, confidential, and staffed by trained people 24/7.

Canada — 24/7 crisis lines

988 Suicide Crisis Helpline

Call or text 988 — any time, anywhere in Canada, in English or French. For anyone thinking about suicide or worried about someone who is.

Kids Help Phone

For children & youth. Call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868. 24/7, confidential.

Hope for Wellness Help Line

Culturally grounded support for Indigenous peoples across Canada. Call 1-855-242-3310 or use the online chat, 24/7.

Québec

1 866 APPELLE — 1-866-277-3553 — for suicide crisis support in Québec, 24/7.

Outside Canada

United States: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
Anywhere else: find a local helpline at findahelpline.com, or call your local emergency number.

When you're ready and it's not an emergency, Bremo can help you find ongoing care. Get matched with a professional →

Phone numbers are provided for convenience and may change. If a number doesn't connect, dial your local emergency services.
