Support, any time

If you need help now.

Reaching out is a good and brave thing to do. Bremo helps you find ongoing care, but it can't respond in the moment — so if you need support urgently, the people below can, day or night.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency number straight away — in Canada and the US that's 911.

Someone to talk to, right now

  • Canada & US — the 988 Suicide & Crisis line. Call or text 988, any time, free and confidential.
  • Young people in Canada — Kids Help Phone. Call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
  • Indigenous peoples in Canada — the Hope for Wellness line. Call 1-855-242-3310, with online chat also available.
  • Elsewhere in the world — you can find a local helpline at findahelpline.com, or contact your local emergency services.

When you're ready and things are calmer, Bremo can help you find someone to talk to regularly. Begin here, whenever suits you.

Phone numbers are shared in good faith and can change. If one doesn't connect, please contact your local emergency services.
