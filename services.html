What we help with

The whole spectrum of care.

You don't have to know exactly what you need. Pick what's closest, and our intake takes it from there — one form covers everything below.

Get matched →

🧠 Mental health & therapy

Licensed psychotherapists, counsellors and psychologists for anxiety, depression, stress and burnout, trauma and PTSD, grief and loss, OCD, and more. Modalities include CBT, DBT, EMDR, ACT and psychodynamic approaches.

🥗 Nutrition & dietetics

Registered dietitians for chronic conditions (diabetes, heart health, IBS), disordered eating and intuitive eating, sports and performance nutrition, prenatal and pediatric nutrition, and building sustainable everyday habits.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family & couples therapy

Marriage, couples and family therapists for communication, conflict, separation and blended families, parenting alignment, and reconnecting after a hard season.

🤝 Social work & support

Registered social workers for counselling, system navigation and advocacy, case management, housing and benefits support, and major life transitions.

🌱 Child & youth

Specialists in child development, play therapy, youth and teen counselling, school and behavioural support, and coaching for parents.

🧭 Wellness & coaching

Support for sleep, chronic stress, addiction and recovery, and health coaching. Coaches complement clinical care — where a clinical need shows up in your intake, we route you to a licensed clinician instead.

Not sure which fits?

That's the most common answer. Describe what's going on and we'll point you to the right kind of professional.
