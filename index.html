How it works

Finding care, made calm.

Step one

Tell us what's on your mind

A short, private note in your own words. As much or as little as you like.

Step two

We find the right fit

We consider your needs, preferences and pace, then introduce you to a licensed professional who suits.

Step three

You begin, at your pace

Meet, see how it feels, and start — online or in person. If it isn't right, we'll introduce you to someone else.

Everyone deserves someone to talk to. We make the finding simple.
